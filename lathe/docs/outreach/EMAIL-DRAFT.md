## Version A — refurbisher / computer-reuse charity

**Subject:** three of your dead laptops, working, free, no commitment

> Hello —
>
> I build custom Linux images for organisations with piles of laptops that Windows has abandoned. I would
> like to do three of yours, free, and you keep them whether or not anything comes of it.
>
> What is different about it: the image contains only what you asked for. If you need four applications,
> the machine has four applications — the rest is deleted from the image, not hidden in a menu. It gets
> rebuilt and patched every night, so it is still current in four years.
>
> The catch, stated up front: **Windows programs do not come across.** Files, bookmarks, Wi-Fi and
> printers do, and we verify every file by hash before touching the disk. If the machines need Windows
> software, this is the wrong product and I will say so.
>
> I am new and have no customers to point you at. What I do have is that every configuration is a public
> file in a public repository — if I vanish, you rebuild the exact same operating system from it without
> me. That is the whole reason to risk three dead laptops on a stranger.
>
> Worth a twenty-minute call?

## Version B — school / nonprofit in Google Workspace

**Subject:** the laptops in your storeroom that Windows stopped supporting

> Hello —
>
> If you have a pile of laptops from around 2012–2018 that will not take current Windows, I would like to
> bring three of them back — free, and you keep them regardless.
>
> Since your staff and students already work in a browser, most of what makes this hard does not apply to
> you. The machines would boot straight into what they actually need, in your language, locked down the
> way you want them, and nothing else on them at all.
>
> Two things I would rather you heard from me than found out later:
> **Windows programs do not transfer** — if anything runs a Windows-only application, that machine should
> keep Windows. And a few of the older machines need one firmware setting changed by hand; I will know
> which before I start.
>
> Files, bookmarks, saved Wi-Fi and printers do transfer, and every file is verified by hash before
> anything on the original disk is changed.
>
> I am new at this and I am not going to pretend otherwise. Every configuration I build is a public file —
> if I disappear, you can rebuild your machines from it without me.
>
> Could I take three from the pile?

## Notes for the human before sending

- **Do not add a savings figure.** "Saves you £X per machine" is the obvious thing to write and we cannot
  evidence it. §4.4.
- **Do not say "trusted by schools".** We are trusted by nobody yet. Saying so is the fastest way to be
  worth distrusting.
- The line that does the work in both versions is *"Windows programs do not come across"*, placed early
  and voluntarily. It costs some replies. The replies it costs were never going to become pilots, and the
  ones it keeps arrive already knowing the hardest thing about the product.
