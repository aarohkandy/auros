## The shape of the constraint, in one line

We may not claim a customer, a saving, a percentage, a comparison, a guarantee, a licence grant, or any
frequency word — which removes roughly every sentence in a normal B2B marketing site and leaves
exactly one thing standing: **measurements, including the ones that make us look bad.**

That is a narrow place to work and it happens to be the right one for this market. The buyer is an IT
person who has been oversold for twenty years and whose actual question is *will this create work for
me*. Nothing on the forbidden list would have answered that question anyway.
