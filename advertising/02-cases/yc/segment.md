# SEGMENT (YC S11) — open-source the small piece

**Steal: 6 · Fit: 5**

---

## The play

Segment open-sourced `analytics.js` — the client library, the small visible piece — and launched on
Hacker News. Developers adopted the library, then bought the service behind it. The free thing was
genuinely useful standalone and led naturally to the paid thing.

## Why it scores low for us, and why it is still here

**It scores low because D30 closed this door.** Everything is all rights reserved. The open-core play
requires something to open, and the decision not to is the owner's, recorded, and not for
re-litigation here.

**It is still here for two reasons.**

First, it is the clearest illustration of what `BLOCKED.md` B9 is really asking. The $0 self-serve tier
*was* this play — the small visible piece, free, leading to the paid thing — and D30 removed the
product from it. B9's option 3 (*open-source only `auros-recipes`*) is literally the Segment shape.
Advertising's view, which is not the deciding view, is in
[`../../00-constraints/human-decisions.md`](../../00-constraints/human-decisions.md) **A2**.

Second, there is a version of this that costs no licence at all: **open the data, not the code.**
`hardware/compat.tsv` can be published as a freely usable dataset while every line of our tooling stays
proprietary. That gets the adoption-then-purchase dynamic, the goodwill with the End of 10 coalition,
and the citability that made Backblaze's dataset an asset — without granting a single right to our
code.

**That is the Segment play, run on the one asset where it costs us nothing**, and it is what
[`../../04-plan/STRATEGY.md`](../../04-plan/STRATEGY.md) proposes.

## The HN launch, specifically

Worth naming so it can be deliberately deferred. A Hacker News launch is free, reaches technical
people, and **is the single most dangerous channel available to us** — a proprietary Linux product
built on Fedora, with no customers and an unreleased image, posted to an audience that enjoys exactly
that kind of audit.

Not never. Not before Gates 1–3 and not before the 25 licence-grant instances are fixed. See
[`../../03-analysis/what-would-make-this-fail.md`](../../03-analysis/what-would-make-this-fail.md).
