## The finding

**Most celebrated YC growth stories presume a product and an audience.** Strip those away and only four
survive: manual onboarding (Stripe), unscalable first customers (Airbnb), selling through an
intermediary (Gusto), and long-tail pages backed by something real (Zapier).

Three of those four are already latent in our own documents — `PILOT-OFFER.md` is a Collison
installation, the recipe conversation is Airbnb's photography, and `hardware/compat.tsv` is Zapier's
integration pages. **The spec got there without naming the cases.** What is missing is Gusto: nobody has
written down who the intermediary is. That is [the refurbisher](../adjacent-market/refurbishers.md).
