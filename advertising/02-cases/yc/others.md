# THE REST OF THE YC SET, AND WHY THEY SCORE LOW

Kept short deliberately. Each is a well-documented growth story that **does not survive our
constraints**, and knowing why is more useful than a full file on each.

The constraint that kills most of them: **they needed users, money, or a shipped product to start.**
We have none of the three.

| Company | The famous play | Why it does not transfer |
|---|---|---|
| **Dropbox** | Referral programme, demo video on Digg | Consumer viral loop. Our buyer is one IT person per organisation; there is nobody to refer to |
| **DoorDash** | Founders delivered the food | Same lesson as [Airbnb](airbnb.md), already captured. No extra insight |
| **Rippling** | Aggressive comparative advertising against incumbents | `comparative-superiority` forbids it, and we have run no measurement against anyone |
| **Brex** | Billboards, heavy paid spend | §4.6. No budget, and no product to spend against |
| **Deel** | Enormous outbound SDR motion | Requires a sales team and a product that ships today |
| **Retool** | Bottom-up developer adoption inside enterprises | No self-serve surface; our product is installed on physical machines by arrangement |
| **Vanta** | Compliance as the wedge, partner-led | **Worth revisiting later.** A school's unpatched-machine problem *is* a compliance problem. Too early: needs a product and an auditor relationship |
| **Webflow** | Showcase of user-built sites | Needs users and visually shareable output. A removal report is not a portfolio piece — though the configurator panel is the closest thing we have |
| **Ironclad / Front / Lattice** | Category creation via analyst relations and events | Expensive, slow, and requires customers to reference |

## The two worth returning to when we have a product

- **Vanta's compliance wedge.** *"An unpatched machine on a school network is a decision somebody has to
  defend"* is already the second sentence of our landing page. That is a compliance sale and we have not
  followed it through — there may be a real angle via safeguarding and data-protection obligations
  rather than via IT budget. Needs research we have not done.
- **Webflow's showcase**, applied to the configurator. Spec §6D says the live `recipe.yaml` panel is
  *"the single most persuasive object on the site"*. A shareable URL for a configuration somebody built
  is the nearest thing to a portfolio piece this product can have, and it costs a route, not a campaign.

## The general finding

Nearly every celebrated YC growth story is a **distribution** story that presumes a product and an
audience. At Gate 1 with no customers, the applicable cases are not the growth ones — they are
[Stripe's manual onboarding](stripe.md), [Airbnb's unscalable first hundred](airbnb.md), and
[Gusto's intermediary channel](gusto.md).

The cases that actually fit us best are not in this folder at all. They are in
[`../data-as-marketing/`](../data-as-marketing/), and the reason is that **publishing a measurement is
the only marketing act available to a company with nothing to point at yet.**
