## The distinction that decides which of these to copy

**Transparency about the work** produces something the reader can use — a dataset, a post-mortem, a
decision log. It compounds and it is checkable.

**Transparency about the company** produces an article. It was novel in 2013 and is not now.

We have an unusual amount of the first kind already written — `DECISIONS.md`, `BLOCKED.md`,
`PROGRESS.md`, `GATE.md` — and nothing of the second kind worth publishing.

## The Plausible finding, which is the important one

Our ChromeOS Flex problem is Plausible's Google Analytics problem: a free, dominant, already-installed
incumbent owned by Google.

Their answer was to find the axis where the incumbent's **business model**, not its backlog, prevents
it from following. For us there are two, both already central to the spec:

1. **Per-organisation subtraction** — an image built for one organisation with a measured removal
   report. ChromeOS is one OS for everybody; this is the opposite of their economics.
2. **An honest compatibility record including the failures.** Google publishes the certified half. A
   platform vendor does not publish what is broken about itself.

Neither was chosen to outflank Google. That they do is worth knowing when deciding what the front page
argues about.
