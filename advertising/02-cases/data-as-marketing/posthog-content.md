# POSTHOG — the content engine

**Steal: 8 · Fit: 7**

---

## The play

PostHog's primary growth channel is content: deep technical writing by people with engineering
backgrounds, about engineering decisions and real lessons from building the product
([PostHog handbook](https://posthog.com/handbook/growth/marketing/blog)). Their tutorials and academy
content drove roughly **40% year-on-year organic traffic growth** by 2026
([Contrary Research](https://research.contrary.com/company/posthog)).

Roughly **70% of early growth came from recommendation and 30% from inbound content marketing**
([How They Grow](https://www.howtheygrow.co/p/how-posthog-grows-the-power-of-being)).

They also publish the handbook, compensation bands, strategy documents and **failed experiments** —
which they connect explicitly to reducing vendor-lock anxiety.

## The three things worth taking

**1. The 70/30 split is the most useful number in this file.** Even for a company whose defining
channel is content, most early growth came from **recommendation**. Content did not replace word of
mouth; it gave word of mouth something to point at.

For us that reorders the plan: the pilot programme (which produces people who recommend) is the primary
channel, and published measurements are what makes the recommendation land. Not the other way round.

**2. Written by the people who did the work.** The credibility is in the byline. This is available to us
and nearly free — our decision log is already written in that voice, by the person who made the
decision, at the time.

**3. Publishing failed experiments is a deliberate trust mechanism**, not an accident of transparency.
Same play as [Cloudflare's post-mortems](cloudflare-postmortems.md), applied to product bets rather than
outages. `DECISIONS.md` is full of these already.

## Where it does not map

PostHog sells to developers, who find products by reading. **Our buyer is an overworked IT person at a
school**, who finds products by asking the person who solved this for them last time — which is again
an argument for reaching them through [an intermediary](../yc/gusto.md).

The content channel reaches the intermediary. It does not reach the school directly, and a plan that
expects it to will show good traffic numbers and no pipeline.

## The concrete adaptation

PostHog writes for the person who will use the product. **We should write for the person who will be
asked about it** — the refurbisher's technical lead, the MSP, the volunteer, the one competent friend.
That reader wants exactly what we have: measurements, failure modes, and the honest edges.
