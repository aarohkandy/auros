## The plan in three lines

1. **Make `hardware/compat.tsv` the top of the funnel.** A dataset nobody else will publish, that
   compounds, that answers the search the buyer is already typing, and that is honesty-gate-safe by
   construction.
2. **Sell through refurbishers, not to schools.** They hold the pile, their stock has no OS path at
   all, they decide in days, and each one is a channel to dozens of schools.
3. **Make the published record of our own mistakes the trust argument**, because every conventional
   trust signal is unavailable to us by definition and this one is already written.

## The bottleneck

Not creative, not budget, not engineering. **Three laptops and one phone call**, and both need a human.
