## Days 1–30 — fix what is false, start what is slow

### [§9] The three that block everything

1. **Get the wind-down terms reviewed, then fix the 25 claims and all three outreach instances.**
   (**A3**, and `BLOCKED.md` B11 — the wind-down entry.) Both emails and the one-pager close on a right
   D30 withdrew, and the gate misses one of the three. The replacement is only true once
   `docs/legal/WIND-DOWN-TERMS.DRAFT.md` is real, so the lawyer comes first and the copy second.
   Replacement wording: [`messaging.md`](messaging.md) §1. **Nothing may be published or sent until this
   closes.**
2. **One refurbisher conversation.** (**A1**) One question: *"how many machines are in your warehouse
   right now that you cannot sell because they will not take Windows 11?"* A number confirms the wedge.
   No answer kills it. Twenty minutes, and it is the highest-value twenty minutes available to this
   company.
3. **Three laptops in the door.** (`BLOCKED.md` B5) Reframed: this is the **marketing** bottleneck, not
   only the Gate 5 one. Zero rows means zero channel.

### [open] Free, and nobody's permission required

4. **Point the honesty gate at `docs/outreach`.** It has never scanned it, which is how two emails to
   real people kept a claim the website was audited for. One line in `verify`:
   [`../00-constraints/honesty-gate-for-marketing.md`](../00-constraints/honesty-gate-for-marketing.md).
5. **Land VM rows in `compat.tsv`** for every QEMU hardware profile, marked `source=vm` with physical
   columns honestly empty. `tools/compat-lint.mjs` already prevents them pretending otherwise. It
   proves the pipeline and the page template before real hardware arrives.
6. **Write the model-page template**, generated from the table. No pages ship until rows exist.
7. **Draft the two published ladders** — Microsoft's ESU prices and ours, each sourced and dated, with
   no adjective between them. This is spec §6D's mandated replacement-cost comparison, in the only form
   `savings-figure` permits.
8. **Check the three market unknowns** in [`../01-market/README.md`](../01-market/README.md): ESU uptake
   among target schools, regional pricing, and whether refurbishers have already solved this.

### [§9] Cheap, decaying, worth deciding now

9. **The code-signing certificate.** (**A5**) ~$10/mo. SmartScreen reputation accrues over time, so it
   is worth strictly less every month it is deferred. It is the largest conversion loss in the funnel
   and nobody had costed it that way.
10. **Resolve B9, the $0 tier.** (**A2**) It is the trust argument as much as a price.

### Explicitly not doing in this window

**Nothing for 2026-10-13.** Consumer ESU ends inside this window and there will be coverage. We have
nothing to launch and an announcement into it is the top failure mode with better timing.

## Days 61–90 — make it repeatable

19. **[gate] Configurator live and shareable.** (Gate 4) Spec §6D calls the live `recipe.yaml` panel
    *"the single most persuasive object on the site"*. A shareable URL for a configuration is the
    closest thing this product has to a portfolio piece.
20. **[§9] First pilot, qualified.** Enforce the question — *"what would you do with three more working
    computers next week?"* No answer, no pilot. A pilot in a cupboard costs more than one that never
    happened.
21. **[open] Technical pieces 2 and 3**, drawn from D8 (signature enforcement that verifies nothing) and
    D21 (upstream garbage-collects the digest you pinned). Both are already written in `DECISIONS.md`;
    the work is editorial.
22. **[open] `compat.tsv` cadence.** A fixed interval for the summary, with an update every time a
    machine is touched. Take Supabase's cadence, leave the format.
23. **[§9] MSP conversations.** The second intermediary, after refurbishers.
24. **[open] Re-read the claims ledger against reality.** Put a date on it. The 25 happened because
    nothing forced this, and the interval between re-readings is the company's exposure window.

## The one-line version

**Month 1: stop being wrong.** **Month 2: get the first rows and the first conversation.**
**Month 3: make both repeatable.**

Everything else waits for a product, and the two things that unblock all of it — **three laptops and
one phone call** — are not engineering problems.
