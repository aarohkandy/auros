## What the table says

**Two channels are open today and they are the same channel**: publish what we have measured, and let
the people already searching for it arrive. Everything else needs a human to approve an email, a
listing, a claim or a spend.

That is not a constraint to route around. It is the reason the plan is a dataset rather than a
campaign — and it happens to be the highest-scoring option anyway.

## The three that score 9–10 and are blocked on a human

**Refurbisher outreach (10)**, **pilots (9)** and the **decision record (9)** are the whole business.
Two are blocked on §9 and the third is blocked on nothing but somebody adding a link.

`BLOCKED.md` B4 already says it: *"Nothing in spec §6 produces a nonprofit... Lead time is human, not
computational."* The scorecard extends that — **lead time on the three best channels is human**, and
computation cannot shorten it.

## The two worth starting early because they are slow

- **TechSoup** — months of qualification before it can convert anything, which is exactly why starting
  it late is the error. A nonprofit going there to find discounted technology is our segment-2 buyer
  arriving pre-validated.
- **Model-page search ranking** — also months, and also starts the moment the first row lands.

Both are cheap, both are slow, and both are worth more the earlier they begin. Everything else can wait
for a product.

## What is deliberately absent

No paid acquisition of any kind, at any stage in this plan. Not because of §4.6 — because the queries
this buyer types have no commercial intent yet (*"does linux work on latitude e6430"* is a research
query, not a buying one) and the ones that do are too rare to bid on.

**Paid acquisition here would buy clicks from people who cannot buy**, which is the most expensive
possible way to learn what the compatibility table tells us for free.
