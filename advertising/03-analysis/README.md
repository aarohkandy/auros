## The three conclusions

**1. The constraints point the same way the evidence does.** We cannot claim customers, savings,
comparisons or guarantees; and the highest-scoring cases in the library
([Backblaze](../02-cases/data-as-marketing/backblaze-drive-stats.md),
[Cloudflare](../02-cases/data-as-marketing/cloudflare-postmortems.md),
[GitLab](../02-cases/yc/gitlab.md)) are all *publish the measurement, including the bad parts*. The
honesty gate is not a handicap on this strategy. It is the strategy's enforcement mechanism.

**2. The best assets already exist and are unlinked.** `DECISIONS.md`, `BLOCKED.md`, `PROGRESS.md`,
`GATE.md` and `LICENSING.md` are public, unusually good, and have never been pointed at a buyer. The
one that is missing — `hardware/compat.tsv` — is empty and blocked on three laptops.

**3. The scarce resource is not money or attention. It is a human's permission.** The three
highest-scoring channels are all blocked on §9, and `BLOCKED.md` B4 already named the reason: lead time
here is human, not computational. The plan is therefore written in two halves — what proceeds with no
approval, and what waits — in [`../04-plan/`](../04-plan/).
